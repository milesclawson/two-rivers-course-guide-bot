The MIT license in this repository does **not** apply to them. If you are an owner and want something changed or removed, please open an issue.

- **Minnesota college admission information** in `knowledge/colleges_mn.md` was gathered from the colleges' and the State of Minnesota's official websites on November 12, 2026 (URLs are listed in the file) and paraphrased.
- Staff names and phone numbers/emails included are those the school and program websites publish.

Information may be out of date. Verify with the school counselors and the colleges themselves.
