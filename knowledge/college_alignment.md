# College alignment: what the 2026-27 Planning Guide says about preparing for different kinds of colleges (guide pp. 96-97)

This is a GENERAL guide, not any one college's rules. College entrance requirements vary and change; students should check each college's own website, Naviance, and meet with their counselor (the guide says exactly this). The guide's chart does not rank every college. Only the colleges named below appear in the guide; for any other college, do NOT guess its tier. Say the guide doesn't place it and suggest the college's website, Naviance or the counselor.

Notes from the guide's text (p. 96): As a general rule, college-bound students should take 4 years of math and 4 years of science, and are strongly encouraged to take advanced-level language arts and social studies and 2-4 years of a world language. Most Minnesota colleges require students to be in the upper half of their class and/or perform satisfactorily on the ACT or SAT. Colleges look favorably on challenging courses, good grades, co-curricular and athletic involvement, and volunteer or leadership roles. For vocational/technical college and armed forces training, a good math background (algebra, geometry, advanced algebra) is strongly recommended, plus good communication skills (reading, writing, speaking, listening); employers care about attendance, tardiness and effort. Entrance assessments are on guide pp. 101-102; NCAA eligibility is on pp. 104-105; college credit options on pp. 98-100.

Caveats for the bot: (1) The chart's test scores and class ranks are general guideposts from the guide and may be out of date; never use them to tell a student they "won't get in." Policies such as test requirements change, so send students to each college's admissions page. (2) The chart says "Physical Science" for science; Two Rivers' Class of 2029+ takes Earth and Space Science instead, so the student should ask their counselor how a college counts it. (3) Two Rivers graduation requires 3 math and 3 science credits, so a 4th year of math or science for selective colleges is an add-on.

## How Two Rivers courses line up (use course info from the knowledge base for details)
- **Math years 4+:** after Algebra II, options include Analysis (0461/0462), AP Precalculus (0473/0474), AP Statistics (0463/0464), CIS College Algebra Through Modeling (0465/0466), AP Calculus AB/BC (0481-0484).
- **Science years 3-4:** Biology plus Chemistry or Physics; extra options include AP Biology, AP Chemistry, AP Environmental Science, Human Anatomy & Physiology, CIS physics courses.
- **Second language:** Spanish (I-V, Spanish for Native Speakers, CE IV-V), French (through V/CIS), German (through IV/CIS), Beginning Ojibwe CE (see course entries).
- **Honors/AP/CE:** Advanced Language Arts, Advanced History, AP and CE/CIS courses; weighted GPA 1.2 multiplier per the guide.
- **Activities:** the guide encourages them (principal's letter and the activities section); Activities office handles sports and club registration.
- **Technical college or trades:** Math for Trades (DCTC, grade 12), TriDistrict CAPS, tech ed courses, Work-Based Learning.
- **Credit saving:** AP score table (ap_scores_mn_colleges.md) and the college credit guide in the addendum.
