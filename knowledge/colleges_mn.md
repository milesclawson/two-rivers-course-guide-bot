- Anything marked "Not found on official pages" is UNKNOWN. Never fill it in from memory. Items marked "(search-snippet)" were not fully verified; present them with extra caution ("worth double-checking").

Two Rivers graduation already requires 4 English, 3 math, 3 science, 1 arts; many colleges above want a 4th year of math, 2+ years of one world language, and rigor (honors/AP/CE).

# Part A: Minnesota college freshman admission requirements
Date fetched: 2026-11-12. Official pages only; paraphrased. Pages often do not name an admission year; noted where known.

## 1. University of Minnesota Twin Cities
- **Sources:** https://admissions.tc.umn.edu/admissions/freshman-admission-university-minnesota-twin-cities ; https://admissions.tc.umn.edu/hs-counselor-guidebook/course-and-test-requirements ; https://admissions.tc.umn.edu/advanced-placement-course-awards ; https://admissions.tc.umn.edu/admissions/freshman-admission/frequently-asked-questions-about-freshman-admission
- **Test policy:** Test-optional, scores not required, holistic review either way; choice is final once submitted. Pages conflict on end date: main page says through fall 2027, counselor guide/FAQ say through fall 2029. Confirm with admissions.
- **Required courses (no admission year named):** English 4; math 4 (elementary algebra, geometry, intermediate algebra; 4th year calculus preferred, or precalc/analysis); science 3 (biological + physical, with lab; Biological Sciences expects biology+chemistry, Science & Engineering expects biology, chemistry, physics); social studies 3 (history and geography); world language 2 same language (not needed if English is first language); visual/performing arts 1.
- **Recommended:** Math and science all four years; admitted students typically exceed minimums; rigor (honors, AP, IB, college-level) is weighed.
- **Admitted profile:** Not found on pages read (page points to z.umn.edu/AcademicProfile; not verified).
- **Deadlines:** Early Action I Nov 1 (decision by Jan 31), EA II Dec 1 (by Feb 28), Regular Jan 1 (by Mar 31); enrollment confirmation May 1. Page does not name a year (copyright 2026, likely fall 2027 cycle).
- **Credit:** AP generally credit at 3+, some exams need 4 or 5 (e.g., Chemistry, Economics, English Lit, Psychology, Statistics), Music Theory 5, Precalculus none. Data shown for Fall 2026 class. PSEO/College in the Schools: Not found on official pages.
- **Programs:** Science & Engineering expects physics too; other competitive-major prep: Not found on official pages.
- **Direct Admissions:** Not found on official pages (UMN pages read do not mention it).

## 2. University of Minnesota Duluth
- **Sources:** https://admissions.d.umn.edu/apply/first-year-students ; https://admissions.d.umn.edu/apply/first-year-students/requirements (current as of Nov 11, 2026) ; https://evcaa.d.umn.edu/credit-nationally-recognized-exams-undergraduate-students ; https://pseo.d.umn.edu/umd-pseo-handbook
- **Test policy:** Test-optional; review considers GPA, grade trends, rigor, optional personal statement.
- **Required courses:** English 4 (writing, literature, speech); math 4 (two years algebra, one intermediate/advanced, plus geometry); science 3 (biological + physical, at least one substantial lab); social studies 3 (one year geography, one American history); arts 1 (with history/critical interpretation). World language: Not found on official pages. (A policy document found via search lists math at 3, not re-verified.)
- **Recommended:** College-prep English and math all four years; competitive programs expect more than minimums.
- **Profile (middle 50%, fall 2025 enrollees):** GPA 3.25-3.85; ACT 21-27.
- **Fall 2027 dates:** Early Action Dec 1; Regular Jan 15 (needed for merit scholarships); confirm May 1; final application Aug 1; rolling decisions from Oct 1.
- **Credit:** AP 3/4/5 can earn credit after evaluation by department; CLEP 50+; IB HL 4-7. PSEO is grade 11-12, within 60 miles; College in the Schools (CITS) is concurrent enrollment; PSEO/CITS credits are transfer credits for other colleges.
- **Programs:** Some programs are capacity-limited and more competitive; specifics Not found.
- **Direct Admissions:** Participates (student must still apply).

- **Recommended extra prep / profile:** Not found on official pages.
- **Deadlines:** Application opens June 15; scholarship priority Jan 15; guaranteed-housing priority Feb 14; comprehensive review deadline July 1 (fall). Years not stated.
- **Credit:** AP, CIS: Not found on official pages. PSEO deadlines July 15 fall / Dec 1 spring (admin.mnsu.edu policy).
- **Programs:** Not found on official pages.
- **Direct Admissions:** Not mentioned on MSU pages; OHE reports list it as a participant (https://ohe.mn.gov/sites/default/files/2025-04/2025%20Direct%20Admissions%20Legislative%20Report.pdf).

## 4. St. Cloud State University

## 5. Winona State University
- **Sources:** https://www.winona.edu/admissions/apply/first-year/ (says online application open for Fall 2027) ; https://www.winona.edu/admissions/apply/transfer-credits/
- **Test policy:** ACT/SAT not required; encouraged for placement and some scholarships.
- **Admission:** GPA 3.0+ with required courses done by end of senior year guarantees admission; lower GPAs reviewed individually. Needs transcript and $20 fee.
- **Required courses:** English 4; math 3; science 3 (1 biological, 1 chemistry or physics, all with labs); social studies 3 (1 US history); 1 year specified electives (world cultures, computer science, arts, music). World language 2 years optional/recommended (ASL counts).
- **Profile:** Not found on official pages.
- **Deadlines:** No fall 2027 date posted on page; decisions typically 2-3 weeks after materials.
- **Credit:** AP 3+ on most exams; Eng Lang & Comp 4-5 gives 3 credits toward ENG 111, a 3 gives elective credit. PSEO credits transfer regardless of grade; College in the Schools handled via transcripts.
- **Programs:** Some degree programs have extra entrance requirements; specifics (e.g., nursing) Not found on official pages.
- **Direct Admissions:** Not mentioned on WSU page.

# 8. Minnesota Direct Admissions
- **Sources:** https://myhighered.mn.gov/direct-admissions-student-center (2026-27) ; https://ohe.mn.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/Direct%20Admissions%20Report_20260310_FINAL_ADA.pdf (search-snippet) ; https://ohe.mn.gov/sites/default/files/2026-08/HS%20Partners%20FAQ%202026-2027_ADA.pdf (search-snippet; PDF unreadable)
- **What:** Voluntary OHE program telling seniors at participating high schools which colleges have pre-agreed to admit them. Over 250 schools; 2026-27 FAQ expects 320+.
- **Who:** Seniors at participating schools. Counselors assign a tier from end-of-junior-year unweighted GPA (five bands, under 2.50 to 3.50-4.00 per 2026 report); must be on track to graduate and in grade-level coursework. Families may opt out.
- **How:** Check school email early senior year for survey link; pick colleges; colleges contact you (2 days-3 weeks); you must still apply to each college (free) and meet deadlines; some colleges ask for extra info. Guarantees general admission, not a program; can be rescinded if not graduating.
- **Participants (53 on page):** includes all seven colleges asked here: St. Thomas, Augsburg, Bethel, Metro State, Inver Hills, DCTC, Century, Normandale. Not participating (FAQ): UMN Twin Cities, Macalester, St. Olaf, Carleton.

# 9. PSEO eligibility, MnTC, Transfer Pathways
- **Sources:** https://education.mn.gov/MDE/dse/ccs/pseo/ ; https://education.mn.gov/MDE/dse/ccs/pseo/pseo/ (10th-grade CTE) ; https://www.minnstate.edu/admissions/transfer/transfer-pathways.html ; https://www.minnstate.edu/admissions/transfer/mntc.html ; https://www.minnstate.edu/board/policy/321.html ; https://www.mntransfer.org (all minnstate/mntransfer pages from search snippets only; certificate errors)
- **PSEO basics (MDE; statute Minn. Stat. 124D.09, verify current year):** Grades 11-12 can take courses full- or part-time if they meet each college's entrance requirements. Grade 10: CTE pathway only; need reading proficiency (8th-grade MCA meets/exceeds) and may take one CTE course first, more with a C or better. Courses must be nonsectarian, not remedial. Cap equals roughly two years if first enrolled in grade 11. Tell district by May 30 (fall) or Oct 30 (spring). Colleges set GPA/test minimums (see items 5-7). Private colleges like St. Thomas may not host PSEO.
- **MnTC:** Statewide 40-credit general education package; must be complete by end of bachelor's; completed at Minnesota State transfers as a block.
- **Transfer Pathways (exist):** AA/AS/AFA degrees at community colleges designed to lead into designated Minnesota State university bachelor's programs within 120 credits; admitted students get junior status with all 60 credits counting; major admission not guaranteed. Reverse transfer lets you earn the AA after transferring. Pathway list: Not found on official pages (page not opened).
