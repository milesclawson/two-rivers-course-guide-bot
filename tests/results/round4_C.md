BOT: That makes sense, and wanting both rigor and a manageable load is a smart thing to plan for. Since you like bio and history and want a competitive 4-year college, here's how credit options differ:

- **CE/CIS:** credit depends on your course grade. It goes on a real college transcript with a college GPA, so a poor grade can hurt. It is generally free.
- **PSEO:** a separate state program on a college campus or online. It is tuition-free but means a heavier schedule and travel.

Two cautions from the guide: some merit scholarships limit transfer credits, and too many credits can force an early major choice.
