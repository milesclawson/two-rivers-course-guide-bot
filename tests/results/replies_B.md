- **Single acceleration:** if you've already earned high school credit for Intermediate Algebra in middle school, the next step is a geometry course in 9th grade (Geometry 0441/0442, or PreAP Geometry 0443/0444).

- PreAP Algebra II with Trigonometry (0431/0432): Homework Guide = 4. Prerequisite is "B+" or better in Intermediate Algebra and PreAP Geometry and/or teacher recommendation.
