STUDENT: ok. can i do PSEO? and what is direct admissions

BOT: PSEO is a state program where you take college classes on a campus or online, tuition-free. The guide says it's open to qualified sophomores, juniors and seniors. The state basics in my notes are narrower: 10th graders can only take career and technical courses, and 11th and 12th graders can take full or part-time classes if they meet the college's entrance rules. You tell your counselor in writing by Oct 30 or May 30. St. Thomas doesn't host PSEO, and at some colleges the nursing courses themselves aren't open to PSEO students, though general-ed classes are. Direct Admissions is a voluntary state program. Seniors at participating high schools find out which colleges have pre-agreed to admit them, based on end-of-junior-year unweighted GPA. You still apply to each college, and it guarantees general admission, not a program like nursing. St. Thomas and UMD take part. The Twin Cities don't. Gustavus and Hamline aren't on my list, so I don't know. Your counselor can say if Two Rivers participates.
